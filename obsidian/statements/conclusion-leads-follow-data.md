# conclusion-leads-follow-data

Every lead sentence on Ops conclusions is computed from the numbers, never fixed text. Overview names whichever of GSC or Area has the higher conversion rate and the lower average process time, and says "but" when they differ. Volume by hour gives the share of mail before or after noon and whether weekday peaks match. Flagged rows names the most common flag reason with its count.

Replaces the old fixed lead "GSC turned a larger share of bookings into shipments, and did it in less time than Area", which was printed even when Area was better.
