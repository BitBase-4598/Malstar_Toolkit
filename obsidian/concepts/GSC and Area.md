# GSC and Area

Two mailbox cohorts on the Ops overview. Each cohort is its own framed column of six cards: bookings, converted, processing, missing shipment, average process time, and average wait.

## Definition
A row is GSC when its mailbox matches one of the five shared addresses, ignoring case. Every other mailbox, including a blank mailbox, is Area. Date and mailbox filters run first, then the split. Process and wait use the existing cleaning rules.
