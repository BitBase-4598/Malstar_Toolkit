# MALSTAR Ops

Ops tab of the MALSTAR Toolkit dashboard. It imports the LCL booking daily-report CSV and shows volumes, process time, and handle wait for a selected date range and mailbox filter.

## Definition
The page is the `Dashboard` tool, Ops sub-page. Metrics live in `backend/services/dashboard_analytics.py`. The UI is `frontend/src/Dashboard.jsx`.
